# Cresolab content direction

Updated 15 October 2026.

## Business scope

The owner clarified that Cresolab operates in **pharmaceutical trading**. This supersedes the former manufacturing-oriented website. The current site focuses on products, requirements, quantities, packaging, availability, and commercial conversations.

## Reference review

- [J. B. Khokhani & Co.](https://www.jbkhokhani.com/): reviewed its separation of product range, trading services, and sourcing enquiries. This informs the product-first navigation and the invitation to discuss requirements outside the listed range. Its API portfolio, international reach, certifications, history, and service claims are not attributed to Cresolab.
- [CMS Group](https://www.thecmsgroup.in/): reviewed its distinction between pharmaceutical wholesale, distribution, and business enquiries. This supports clear trade-oriented positioning. Its distribution infrastructure, scale, technology, and delivery claims are not attributed to Cresolab.

Copy is original. These references inform information architecture, not verification of Cresolab's operations.

## Current editorial decisions

1. Use “pharmaceutical trading” consistently in metadata, company description, navigation, pages, and downloads.
2. Retain the official supplied logo, warm brown palette, existing contact details, and the 18 supplied product records.
3. Replace manufacturing, facility, research, technology-transfer, and export-capability messaging with a simple product and trade enquiry flow.
4. Reduce navigation to Products, How we work, About us, and Trade enquiry. Keep the privacy notice in the footer.
5. Remove generic insight articles and unused corporate sections. Redirect their former URLs so existing links remain useful.
6. Use the existing representative product-pack imagery, with short visible labels and an explanation in the website notice. No facility or laboratory images are used.
7. Discuss availability, minimum quantities, pricing, and documentation per enquiry. Avoid invented service guarantees, certifications, warehousing claims, or delivery schedules.
8. Keep the enquiry form transparent: it prepares a local email draft; the visitor sends it in an email application.
9. Keep public copy short. Do not surface development details or a long approval checklist in the business flow.

The portfolio retains its supplied composition, strength, and packaging data. This update changes business positioning and presentation; it does not independently validate those records.
