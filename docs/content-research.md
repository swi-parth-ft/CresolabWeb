# Cresolab content and design research

Reviewed 31 August 2026. This research informs structure and language; it does **not** verify any Cresolab company or product claims.

## Reference sites

- [Piramal Pharma Solutions](https://www.piramalpharmasolutions.com/): separates dosage-form services, facilities, quality, resources, and partnership information. Adopted the clear paths for technical and commercial audiences, without borrowing its claims or statistics.
- [Siegfried](https://www.siegfried.ch/): describes services and manufacturing scope explicitly and gives company news its own space. Adopted concise service framing and editorial hierarchy, without suggesting comparable scale or capabilities.
- [WHO GMP overview](https://www.who.int/teams/health-product-policy-and-standards/standards-and-specifications/norms-and-standards/gmp): informed a quality discussion centered on documented processes and controls rather than badges.
- [WHO certification scheme](https://www.who.int/teams/regulation-prequalification/regulation-and-safety/regulatory-convergence-networks/certification-scheme): informed the distinction between website portfolio information and product/site/market-specific certification or authorization.
- [WHO GMP questions and answers](https://www.who.int/news-room/questions-and-answers/item/medicines-good-manufacturing-processes): informed the short perspective on quality throughout the manufacturing process.

All new copy is original. Competitor capabilities and performance data are not attributed to Cresolab.

## Editorial decisions

1. Lead with the brand, formulation portfolio, and a clear business action.
2. Organize around the buyer's questions: product, dosage form, manufacturing scope, quality documentation, destination market, and expected volume.
3. Keep the existing 18 product names, generic descriptions, strengths, packaging, and categories as supplied catalog data. Label these as requiring confirmation. Remove unsupported clinical benefit and dosage copy from the public dataset.
4. Replace unverified corporate announcements with sourced, general educational perspectives.
5. Replace unverified trial stages and progress percentages with a development discussion framework.
6. Remove unverified leadership biographies, certification badges, export approval lists, production figures, environmental metrics, and implied current job vacancies.
7. Use representative AI-generated imagery with visible captions and a clear website notice. It must not be presented as photography of Cresolab premises or employees.
8. Do not claim that the contact form sends data. It prepares a local draft, lets the visitor review/copy it, and offers a mailto link. Sending remains the visitor's action in their email app.
